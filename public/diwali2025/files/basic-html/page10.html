                                    Raghuvanshi 2025 - 2026 Happy Diwali10LCNL Diwali Magazine P residents ReportJai Shree Krishna Jai Shree Ram Jai JalaramI am delighted to be writing this report in the 2025/2026 LCNL Raghuvanshi Diwali Magazine. Diwali, the Festival of Lights, marks the triumph of good over evil %u2013 light over darkness. It%u2019s one of my favourite times of the year. Not only do we enjoy lots of great food like mathia, chakri, biscuits, cakes and all those wonderful home-made goodies, but we also get to spend time with family and friends %u2013 some we see often and others only a few times a year. The most important thing, however, is that we are together during Diwali.From an LCNL point of view, Diwali is a very busy time. We have just come out of Navratri and all the joy of spending time with Mataji. At the same time, we were working hard on this magazine. Now our attention turns to Chodpa Poojan on Diwali day, followed by the Diwali Music Night on 24th October, and soon after, Jalaram Jayanti. This variety of events shows the dedication and hard work of everyone at LCNL. I must say, the team this year has been amazing. My presidency has always been about clear communication, transparency, teamwork, and having fun. So far, the team has really demonstrated this, and I hope this stays with LCNL not just in my term as President but in the future too. I would like to say a huge thank you to all the LCNL Committee and volunteers who have helped us so far this year. I look forward to working with you all in the coming months as we prepare to celebrate LCNL%u2019s 50th anniversary next year. Look out for more information on that soon!I also want to thank my family for their support since I became President of LCNL. Rayna has been so understanding of the workload I%u2019ve had, while Riyan and Raani have sometimes had to spend more time with their mummy than we would have liked and they now see DLC as a second home! Mum and Dad, Davain and Deepa have always encouraged me, and the same goes for my in-laws, Rahee, and Paavan too. All of them have helped me be the leader I am today.Without the public %u2013 all of you %u2013 none of our events would matter. Thank you for attending and supporting us. My only request is the same as always: please continue to support our events, and let us know if there are any new events or functions, you%u2019d like us to host. We will do our best to make them happen.The LCNL Website has been redesigned and you will be able to see that we now have the event flyers, more information on all the committees and also a live feed to events held by LCNL at DLC all there at the click of a button. Please do have a look at www.lcnl.org and also sign up for our new mailing list feature to be kept informed about everything we do here at LCNL. This magazine is a testament to the hard work of Dhiru Savani and myself, but the most credit this year goes to Ritesh Thakrar. He has worked tirelessly to gather adverts, articles, and ensure that the magazine has all the glitz and glamour of previous years, if not more. Steve from Liquid Bubble has been invaluable on the design side, and I also thank Sanjiv from Liquid Bubble for his support. Valuable input also came from Dinesh Shonchhatra, Geeta Nathwani, Jeet Rughani, Meena Jasani, Sanjay Rughani, and Neal Vithlani for securing the Prime Minister%u2019s message.Lastly, I wish you all a Happy Diwali and a Happy New Year. May the spirit of Diwali continue to inspire us all to spread light, joy, and harmony.Ronak PawLCNL President 2025-2027
