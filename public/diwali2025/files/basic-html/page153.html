                                    Raghuvanshi 2025 - 2026 Happy Diwali153Swinging into Success: Golf in the Lohana Community By Lord Dolar PopatGolf, often referred to as the %u2018gentleman%u2019s game,%u2019 is a sport known for its elegance, precision, and rich history. Whilst it has a predominantly male global following, it has also found a special place within the ladies of the Lohana community. They have embraced golf as not just a sport but also as a means of strengthening bonds, networking, and cultural preservation. Having been invited many times previously to play in the Annual Lohana Golf Day, it was only the first time this July when I attended and discovered just how many women play the sport, too.The sport may not have been the most traditional within our community, but it has gained significant popularity among its members in recent years. There are many reasons for this increase. First and foremost, golf aligns with the community%u2019s values of discipline, patience, and precision. Making it an attractive choice for many members.My personal love for golf certainly does not fall short. During my time in government, I would often travel to Uganda and Rwanda on business and for each trip I always ensured I made time to play. In fact, for every keen golfer the first thought that comes to mind on all trips is the nearest golf course to our destination. I always ensure when I travel that I take two important things: my passport and my golf kit. Golf in Uganda makes one used to caddies which is rather different from playing in Britain. Here when you strike the ball onto the fairway, great concentration is required as there are no caddies to spot the ball. Thus in Britian, especially courses such as Moor Park, help to develop ones skills.Beyond my personal enjoyment for the sport, it offers an excellent platform for networking and building strong bonds, which reflect the values of our community and country. Allowing members to connect with individuals who they might not have otherwise crossed paths with.Players participate in golf tournaments, charity events, and social gatherings organised by different clubs and associations worldwide. These events provide a unique opportunity to strengthen existing relationships and forge new ones, both within the community and with members of other communities. With tournaments supporting charities such as St Luke%u2019s Hospice that highlight the community%u2019s commitment and dedication to improving our wider society.In addition to its social and networking aspects, golf has played a role in preserving and promoting Lohana culture. Many golf tournaments incorporate elements of traditional dress, music, and barbeque, creating an atmosphere that celebrates the community%u2019s heritage. These events provide an excellent opportunity for younger generations to connect with their roots and learn more about each other. A sport that truly warms inclusivity where people of all ages can come together and play, connecting generations through shared participation.It is worth noting that golf offers health benefits beyond its social and cultural aspects. Golf offers impressive physical benefits through its gentle, low-impact exercise. Research has shown it can be even more effective than walking in reducing cholesterol, blood sugar levels, and blood pressure, particularly in individuals over 65. Additionally, golf%u2019s ability to help one%u2019s loneliness is incredible. With the patience and discipline, it requires, it is very much a mind game too.Golf has evolved from being a sport to a symbol of unity, charity, and cultural preservation. Through the sport, Lohana%u2019s have found a way to connect and give back to society while celebrating their rich cultural heritage. Although golf has brought numerous benefits to our community, challenges persist. The high cost associated with golf, including outfits and equipment, can act as a significant barrier. Efforts are underway to make the sport more accessible through scholarships, sponsorships, and community initiatives. I was delighted to be part of the Lohana North London Golf Competition at Moor Park Golf Club. Which was sponsored by Vijay Parikh, managing director at Harold Benjamin Solicitors along with many other firms from the community.The sport%u2019s growth and significant traction within the community highlight the adaptability and openness of the Lohana diaspora as they continue to blend their traditional values with the opportunities of the modern world. Golf is not just a game for the community, it is a means of strengthening bonds and building a brighter future for generations to come.As golf continues to flourish within our community, it serves as a testament to the enduring power of sport in bringing people together, fostering positive change, and preserving British Lohana values. My only warning to readers of this article is golf is an additive drug, but a beautiful addiction.
