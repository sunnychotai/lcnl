                                    Raghuvanshi 2025 - 2026 Happy Diwali20On this sacred festival of Diwali, I pray that Bhagawan Shri Ram%u2019s grace fills your homes with health, joy, peace, and prosperity. Diwali celebrates the victorious return of Bhagawan Ram to Ayodhya, where the people lit countless lamps to welcome Him and usher in Ramrajya, an era of dharma, integrity, justice, and harmony. Today, especially as the long-awaited Ram Janmabhoomi Mandir has been built in Ayodhya, we are called even more deeply to bring forth this spirit of Ramrajya in our lives and communities, living by the timeless teachings and principles of Bhagawan Ram. True darkness is not only the absence of light but also the shadows of ignorance, greed, division, and disconnection from our own true nature and from Mother Nature. These are not separate, for what we do to our rivers, forests, and air reflects our inner state. When we forget our intrinsic harmony with Nature, we lose connection with our bodies and minds as well. Conscious living means living in alignment, with ourselves, with each other, and with the Earth. Diwali Blessings/PARMARTHNIKETAN WWW.PARMARTH.ORG Dear Divine Souls of the Lohana Community North London,Bhagawan Sri Ram Himself spent 14 years in exile in the forests, living simply and in tune with Nature. His life reminds us that while we may not go into exile, we each need to spend time every year immersed in Nature, walking in the mountains, trekking, meditating, practicing yoga, and living simply and sustainably. Time in Nature resets us in a way that nothing else can, reconnecting us to our families, our health, and our deeper selves. Across the globe, Mother Earth is crying, through rising temperatures, floods, and fires, and humanity is facing mounting challenges to health and well-being. Bhagawan Ram%u2019s life itself was an embodiment of balance, righteousness, and harmony, principles that are not only spiritual but also the foundation of healthy, long, and meaningful living. In today%u2019s age of Artificial Intelligence, it is even more essential to preserve and tune into Nature%u2019s Intelligence and Rishi%u2019s Intelligence, the wisdom that flows from Mother Nature and the guidance passed down by our Rishis. The mantras and teachings of our sages are timeless blueprints for a healthy, sustainable, and spiritually awakened civilization. These sanskaras and this culture are the foundation of our collective well-being, and they remind us that technology may serve us, but true intelligence comes from living in tune with dharma and Nature. This Diwali, let us celebrate not with overindulgence or excess, but with awareness, balance, and inner light. The true celebration of Diwali is an inner awakening, not just external festivity. Let us pledge to make our lives a light%u2014by reducing waste, protecting our rivers and forests, choosing sustainable lifestyles, nurturing our health, and teaching our children to live in harmony with Mother Earth. Together, we can create a %u201cGreen Ramrajya,%u201d a world guided by goodness, responsibility, and reverence for all of life. May the lamps of Diwali not only brighten your homes but also illuminate the path of conscious living, inner awakening, health, and global harmony. Let us be the light that heals, uplifts, and transforms our world. With deepest love and blessings, In the service of God and Humanity, Swami Chidanand Saraswati swamiji@parmarth.com www.PujyaSwamiji.org & www.Parmarth.org /PujyaSwamiji
